# DIM 02 — Business Rules, Search, Reporting & UX Audit Brief (UK/IE scope)

**Scope:** United Kingdom + Republic of Ireland only. No EU-wide recommendations unless marked *Future (v2.0+)*. No SQL/migrations — review and recommend only.

| # | Finding | Evidence (table.column) | Severity | Effort | Migration Risk | Implement |

## C) Search Readiness

**Verdict: NOT search-ready for production.** The schema dump shows constraints only — **no indexes at all are visible**, and specifically no `pg_trgm` GIN indexes, no `tsvector`/full-text columns, and no composite indexes on the tenant-scoped query patterns every list view will use.

| # | Finding | Evidence | Severity | Effort | Risk | Implement |
|---|---------|----------|----------|--------|------|-----------|
| C1 | **No trigram/full-text indexing** on the entities users will search: `organizations.name`, `suppliers.name`/`vat_number`/`company_number`, `facilities.name`/`postcode`, `customer_documents.file_name`, `report_generation_queue.report_name`, `messages.content`, `users.email`, `internal_tasks.task_title`, `user_feedback.title`. Recommend pg_trgm GIN on name/email/reference columns + tsvector on `messages.content`, `customer_documents.organization_notes`, `internal_tasks`. | entire schema — zero index definitions | 🔴 | Small–Medium | Low | v1.0 |

## D) UK SECR & Reporting Readiness

SECR requires, for large UK companies/LLPs: **total UK energy use in kWh** (Scope 1+2, incl. transport), **associated GHG emissions (tCO2e)**, **at least one intensity ratio** (e.g., tCO2e/£m turnover, per FTE, per m²), **prior-year comparatives**, **energy-efficiency action narrative**, and alignment to the **financial year** of the Directors' Report. Quoted companies have additional global-energy obligations.

| # | Assessment | Evidence | Severity | Effort | Risk | Implement |
|---|-----------|----------|----------|--------|------|-----------|
| D1 | **kWh energy data: ADEQUATE with caveats.** `emissions_logs.raw_quantity` (numeric) can hold kWh with the unit implied by the DEFRA factor (`defra_conversion_factors.activity_type`), and `units` table exists. Caveats: (a) **no `unit_id`/`unit_code` column on `emissions_logs`** — unit is only inferable via the factor join; SECR kWh totals need an explicit unit to sum safely. (b) No `scope` column on `emissions_logs` — scope comes indirectly via `activity_categories.ghg_protocol_scope` or `document_type_categories.default_scope`; report aggregation will be multi-hop and fragile. Add `unit_code` + denormalized `ghg_protocol_scope` (or activity_category FK) to `emissions_logs`. | `emissions_logs` | 🟠 | Small | Low | v1.0 |
| D2 | **Intensity-ratio denominators: PRESENT.** `organization_metadata` has `annual_revenue`, `average_employees`/`total_employees`, `total_floor_area_sqft`, plus `energy_intensity` (unit-less — specify expected unit or add `energy_intensity_unit`). Good coverage of SECR-accepted denominators. | `organization_metadata` | 🟢 | Small | Low | v1.1 (unit column) |
| D3 | **sqft-only floor area is wrong-side-of-the-channel.** UK property practice commonly uses sq ft, but Irish practice (and SECR-adjacent EPC/BER reporting) uses **m²**; Irish customers (seed user `emma.walsh@greenenergy.ie`) will enter m² into `total_floor_area_sqft`. Recommend either dual columns (`total_floor_area_sqm`) or a `floor_area_unit` column + conversion via `units.conversion_factor`. | `organization_metadata.total_floor_area_sqft`, `occupied_floor_area_sqft` | 🟡 | Small | Low | v1.1 |
| D4 | **Financial-year boundary: PRESENT but duplicated/divergent.** `organizations.financial_year_end` (date) AND `organization_metadata.fiscal_year_start`/`fiscal_year_end` (dates) — two sources of truth for the same SECR reporting period. `report_generation_queue.reporting_year` is an `int4` — cannot represent a non-calendar FY (e.g., FY ending 31 Mar 2025). But `secr_enabled` is a bare bool; no field captures *why* an org is in scope (2-of-3 size criteria) or LLP status (`business_structure` exists as free varchar — constrain it). Adequate for v1.0 eligibility flag; v1.1 add structured SECR-qualification metadata. | `organizations` | 🟡 | Small | Low | v1.1 |
| D6 | **Prior-year comparatives: PRESENT.** `defra_conversion_factors.reporting_year` + `emissions_logs` date ranges + `organizations.default_defra_version` support multi-year data. SECR comparatives are feasible. | factor tables | 🟢 | — | — | — |
| D7 | **Report versioning/approval: PRESENT.** `report_templates`, `report_generation_queue`, `report_versions` (with `is_current`), `report_comments`, `ai_content_history`, `export_history` form a solid reporting spine. Supplier/facility/activity/audit/compliance reports can be built from `suppliers.annual_emissions_scope1/2/3`, `facilities`→`assets`→`emissions_logs`, and the audit-log estate (once B1 view exists) **without redesign**. | reporting tables | 🟢 | — | — | — |

## E) Irish Emission Factor Gap

| # | Finding | Severity | Effort | Risk | Implement |
|---|---------|----------|--------|------|-----------|
| E1 | **🔴 Ireland has no emission-factor set.** The only factor table is `defra_conversion_factors` (UK DESNZ/DEFRA) and the only factor FK in the pipeline is `emissions_logs.defra_factor_id` (also `document_processing_queue.defra_factor_used`, `manual_extraction_items.defra_factor_used`, `organizations.default_defra_version`). Irish practice is to use **SEAI/EPA Ireland emission factors** (e.g., Irish grid electricity factor differs materially from UK grid — using DEFRA's UK grid factor for a Dublin office misstates Scope 2). Alternatively a parallel `seai_conversion_factors` table is acceptable short-term but entrenches the B10 fragmentation. | 🔴 | Medium | Medium (column add + backfill 'DEFRA-DESNZ' default; FK rename deferred) | v1.0 (country/source columns) / v1.1 (SEAI data load) |
| E2 | `system_settings.default_emission_factor_set` (varchar) already anticipates multiple factor sets — the settings layer is ready; the data layer is not. This confirms E1 is a gap, not a design choice. | 🟡 | — | — | — |

## Top Priority Actions (v1.0 gate)

1. 🔴 Add archive/deactivate capability to `organizations` (A1) and soft-delete to `customer_documents` (A3).
2. 🔴 Index baseline: pg_trgm on name/email/reference columns + tenant-scoped composites (C1, C4).
3. 🔴 Irish factor readiness: `factor_set`/`country` on factor table + `eircode` on `facilities` (E1, E3).
4. 🟠 Unique constraints: `suppliers(org,name/vat/company)`, `consultant_clients(consultant_id,organization_id)`, `organization_members(organization_id,user_id)` (A4, A6, A7).
5. 🟠 `file_checksum` SHA-256 on file tables (A5); drop `pending_invites` + one of `notification_delivery`/`notification_delivery_log` (B4, B5).
6. 🟠 `unit_code` + scope denormalisation on `emissions_logs`; single fiscal-year source (D1, D4).
7. **NOT RECOMMENDED (ADR conflicts):** merging the three processing queues (B2), consolidating the 9 log tables (B1), merging users/staff_profiles (B6) — pursue documentation, CHECK constraints, and read-views instead.

**Remaining uncertainty:** The schema dump shows no indexes, triggers, RLS policies, or CHECK constraints — severity ratings for A2/A8/C1/C4 assume none exist elsewhere in the migrations; if RLS/indexes do exist in migration files (not provided), the Search and Business-Rules scores improve but the coherence findings (B-series) and the Irish factor gap (E1) stand regardless.
