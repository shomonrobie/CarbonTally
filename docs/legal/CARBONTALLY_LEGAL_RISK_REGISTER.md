# CarbonTally V3 — Legal Risk Register

**Status:** Draft — for legal review
**Date:** 24 August 2026
**Method:** Read-only product/policy consistency audit (see `CARBONTALLY_POLICY_PRODUCT_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md`). Severity: **P0** = must resolve before commercial launch / before the relevant policy statement can be made; **P1** = should resolve before/around launch; **P2** = improve after launch.

This register identifies risk *based on the evidence available*. Where evidence is insufficient, that is stated rather than assumed. It is not legal advice.

### Register maintenance

- Re-verify provider legal/compliance statements quarterly or on provider notification of change.
- Add an entry to this register whenever a policy or feature changes.
- Close an entry only when the underlying product/contractual state has changed and the affected policy text has been updated.
