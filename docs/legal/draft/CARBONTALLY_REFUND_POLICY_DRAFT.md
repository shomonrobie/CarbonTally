> **Drafting note — IMPORTANT:** CarbonTally does **not** currently collect online payments. The D37 billing surface manages **credits and orders**, not money; there is no payment-provider integration (`[PRODUCT GAP]`). This Refund Policy is therefore drafted as a **framework that takes effect when payments launch**. It does **not** promise any refund capability that the current billing system can operationally support. Monetary-refund language below is conditional on a payment provider being integrated and is marked `[PROPOSED — REQUIRES PRODUCT OWNER / LEGAL APPROVAL]`.

## 1. Current status

1.1 **Until online payments are available, no money is collected and no monetary refunds can be given.** CarbonTally provides the Service on a pre-launch, access-by-arrangement basis. Any credit adjustments in the current system are administrative operations performed by authorised CarbonTally personnel.

1.2 This policy will be updated and take effect in full when paid subscriptions and payment processing launch. You will be informed before any charge is made.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy covers refunds and cancellation for paid subscriptions and one-off processing orders (including prepaid credits) once available. It applies to payments you make to CarbonTally through the Service.

2.2 **Consumer protection:** Where you are a consumer (an individual acting outside a trade, business, craft or profession), UK/EU consumer law — including the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 — may give you cancellation and refund rights beyond this policy. Those rights are not affected by this policy. `[LEGAL REVIEW]`

## 3. Subscription cancellation — [PROPOSED — REQUIRES PRODUCT OWNER / LEGAL APPROVAL]

3.1 You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings or by contacting us.

3.2 Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain access until then.

3.3 **[Proposal]** We will not automatically refund the unused portion of a cancelled subscription period; the choice of pro-rata refund vs. no refund is a commercial decision requiring Product Owner approval.

## 4. Prepaid and unused credits — [PROPOSED — REQUIRES PRODUCT OWNER / LEGAL APPROVAL]

4.1 Plan credits are provided for use during the relevant billing period. Unused credits do not roll over unless your plan says otherwise.

4.2 Credits purchased separately (if offered) will be refunded on a pro-rata basis if you cancel within [·] days of purchase and have not used them.

## 5. Duplicate payments and erroneous charges — [PROPOSED]

5.1 If you are charged twice for the same purchase, or charged an amount you did not authorise, contact us within [·] days; we will verify and refund the erroneous charge in full, in the original form of payment.

## 6. Service failure — [PROPOSED — REQUIRES PRODUCT OWNER / LEGAL APPROVAL]

6.1 If the Service fails materially (for example, a sustained outage that prevents core processing) and that failure is caused by CarbonTally (and not by third-party infrastructure, your systems, or events outside our control), you may request a refund of fees paid for the affected period, or a service credit, as agreed.

6.2 **Third-party outages are excluded.** We are not responsible for outages of the infrastructure providers the Service depends on (see the Terms of Service, Section 7).

## 7. Billing disputes

7.1 If you believe a charge is incorrect, you must contact us within [·] days of the charge. While a dispute is being investigated we will not treat the charge as agreed; if the charge is found to be correct, it remains payable.

## 8. Refunds — how they work — [PROPOSED]

8.1 Refunds (once payments launch) will be issued to the original payment method within [·] business days of approval, except where a different method is agreed.

8.2 In the current pre-payment state, any over-allocation of credits is corrected by credit adjustment, not by monetary refund.

## 9. Trial periods — [PROPOSED — REQUIRES PRODUCT OWNER / LEGAL APPROVAL]

9.1 If a free trial is offered, it starts on account creation and lasts the stated number of days. You will not be charged during the trial unless you opt in. We will seek your confirmation before converting a trial to a paid plan.

## 10. Termination for breach

10.1 If CarbonTally terminates the agreement for your material breach, you are not entitled to a refund of fees already paid, except where required by law.
