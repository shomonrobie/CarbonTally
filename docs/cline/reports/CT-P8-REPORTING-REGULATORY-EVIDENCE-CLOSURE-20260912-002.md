## 4. Exact evidence obtained

### 4.1 UK SECR [V] — source B (official gov.uk guidance, updated 12 Aug 2026)

* **Instrument (verbatim):** *"The **Companies (Directors' Report) and Limited Liability Partnerships (Energy and Carbon Report) Regulations 2018** (the 2018 Regulations) implement the requirements for Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR)."*
* **Disclosure contents (official worked example):** energy consumption used to calculate emissions **(kWh)**; optional breakdown (gas/electricity/transport fuel); **Scope 1** (tCO2e); **Scope 2** (tCO2e); **Scope 3** (business travel in employee-owned vehicles, tCO2e); **total gross emissions** (tCO2e); **intensity ratio** + rationale; **quantification and reporting methodology** (2019 HM Government Environmental Reporting Guidelines + **GHG Reporting Protocol – Corporate Standard** + UK Government Conversion Factors); **measures taken to improve energy efficiency**; and *"the previous year's data must also be reported for comparison purposes."*
* Fuel in **litres** → convert to **kWh** ("the preferred and more accurate data to use").

### 4.2 CSRD phase-in timing [V] — source A (Directive (EU) 2025/794, OJ L 2025/794, 16.4.2025)

* Original phasing (recital 3): large PIEs >500 employees → **FY beginning on/after 1 Jan 2024**; other large undertakings → **FY on/after 1 Jan 2025**; SMEs (excl. micro) etc. → **FY on/after 1 Jan 2026**.
* Amendment: *"the sustainability reporting requirements for those undertakings should be **postponed by two years**"* → **other large undertakings: FY on/after 1 Jan 2027**; **SMEs/SNCIs: FY on/after 1 Jan 2028**.
* **Transposition deadline of the amending directive: 31 December 2025** (Article 3). CSDDD application dates amended in Article 2 (from 26 July 2027 / 2028 / 2029).

### 4.3 Ireland [V] — sources D and E (Irish Statute Book, official)

* **S.I. No. 336/2024 — European Union (Corporate Sustainability Reporting) Regulations 2024**: made by the Minister for Enterprise, Trade and Employment; notice in **Iris Oifigiúil, 9 July 2024**; **comes into operation 6 July 2024**; gives effect to Directive (EU) 2022/2464; amends the **Companies Act 2014**, inserting CSRD-style sustainability-reporting content (business model & strategy incl. resilience; **time-bound targets incl. absolute GHG reduction targets at least for 2030 and 2050**; role/expertise of bodies; policies; incentive schemes; due diligence; principal impacts; principal risks & dependencies; indicators) with a **first-three-financial-years value-chain relief**.
* **S.I. No. 498/2024 — European Union (Corporate Sustainability Reporting) (No. 2) Regulations 2024**: made **1 October 2024** (Iris Oifigiúil, 4 October 2024); amends Companies Act 2014 **ss. 1587, 1594, 1598**; explanatory note states it amends *"further to"* S.I. 336/2024.
