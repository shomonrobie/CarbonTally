# CarbonTally V3 — Draft Pricing Strategy & Competitive Benchmark
## Working Draft — 2026-08-23

> **Status: DRAFT.** This document is for product/commercial review. Prices are proposed starting points, not final commitments.

---

## 1. Executive recommendation

CarbonTally should **not** price like a conventional per-seat SaaS product.

Its cost and value structure is different because the platform provides:

1. Carbon-accounting software.
2. Automated data ingestion and mapping.
3. PDF/image document extraction.
4. Human/manual extraction through CarbonTally and Processing Entities.
5. Emission-factor matching and calculation.
6. Evidence/provenance linking.
7. Document and source-data retention.
8. Reporting and audit support.
9. Consultant portfolio/managed-service capabilities.
10. White-label platform capabilities.

Therefore the recommended commercial model is:

**Platform subscription + processing usage + human extraction + optional professional services.**

This keeps low-volume customers affordable while preventing very high document-processing customers from creating an unsustainable fixed-cost burden.

# 2. Competitive benchmark

## 2.1 CarbonAnalytics

CarbonAnalytics publicly lists:

- Starter: **£69.99/business unit/month**
- Pro: **£299/business unit/month**
- Growth: **£799/business unit/month**

Its plans include Scope 1/2/3 accounting, supplier limits, users, API, exports and automation. Annual billing is discounted. Additional supplier credits are separately priced.

Source: CarbonAnalytics public pricing page.

## 2.2 Carbon Accounting / Ledger-first competitor

Carbonaccounting.ai currently publishes planned pricing of:

- Ledger: **$312/month annually** or $390 monthly
- Compliance: **$1,032/month annually** or $1,290 monthly
- Assurance: **$3,120/month annually** or $3,900 monthly
- Enterprise: custom

Notably, its Ledger tier explicitly includes an **evidence link from every posting to its source line**. This is strategically important for CarbonTally because evidence traceability should be a core product capability rather than an optional add-on.

Source: carbonaccounting.ai public pricing page.

## 2.3 Normative

Normative publicly lists Essential and Premium packages but uses **quote-based pricing**.

Its published feature set includes automated carbon accounting, AI data matching/validation, carbon reporting, data management, value-chain engagement and consultancy/data services.

Source: Normative public pricing page.

## 2.4 Carbon Analytics / co2analytics.com

Another carbon-accounting provider publishes annual plans ranging from:

- Startup: **£360/year**
- Small: **£720/year**
- Medium: **£1,750/year**
- Premium: **£7,500/year**

It also publishes US-dollar plans from approximately $1,249/year upward.

This demonstrates that there is a substantial lower-cost SME segment below enterprise carbon-accounting pricing.

Source: co2analytics.com public pricing page.

## 2.5 CarbonPass

CarbonPass publicly lists:

- Free: £0
- Starter: **£199/year**
- Business: **£499/year**
- Partner: **£1,499/year**

Partner includes unlimited client accounts, unlimited team members, white-label CRPs, partner branding and API access.

This is particularly relevant to CarbonTally's consultant strategy.

Source: CarbonPass public pricing page.

## 2.6 Market conclusion

The market is not converging on one pricing model.

There are roughly three observable approaches:

| Segment | Typical approach |
|---|---|
| SME/self-service | Low annual/monthly subscription |
| Growing/complex companies | £100s/month |
| Enterprise/assurance/advisory | Quote-based, often much higher |

CarbonTally should occupy a **data-processing-first position** between low-cost carbon calculators and high-cost enterprise carbon-accounting/advisory platforms.

# 3. CarbonTally's differentiation

CarbonTally should not primarily sell:

> "A dashboard for calculating your carbon footprint."

It should sell:

> **"A platform that turns messy operational data and source documents into mapped, calculated and traceable carbon data."**

The strongest differentiators are:

- CSV/Excel/JSON ingestion.
- PDF/image invoice extraction.
- Automated extraction.
- Human extraction when automation is insufficient.
- Processing Entity network.
- Automatic emission-factor mapping.
- Calculation engine.
- Validation/QC workflow.
- Exact source-document evidence linkage.
- Document retention.
- Consultant managed-service workflow.
- White-label capability.

This means CarbonTally can charge for **data processing**, not just software access.

|---|---:|

# 10. Human/manual extraction

Manual extraction should be separately priced because it creates a real operational labour cost.

### Proposed starting rates

| Document type | Starting price |
|---|---:|
| Standard invoice, 1–5 pages | **£0.99/document** |
| Complex, 6–15 pages | **£1.99/document** |
| Very complex, 16+ pages | **£3.99/document** |

Normal target SLA could initially be:

**24–48 hours**

Priority processing can carry a premium once actual operational capacity is known.

These are proposed prices, not final prices.

---

# 11. Why manual extraction should be separate

A customer processing 100 documents and a customer processing 50,000 documents should not pay the same total amount for the service.

The subscription pays for the platform.

Processing usage pays for computational workload.

Human extraction pays for human operational workload.

This creates a much safer unit-economics model.

# 12. Future complexity-based manual pricing

Instead of permanently pricing only by PDF count, CarbonTally could eventually use extraction units:

| Complexity | Units |
|---|---:|
| Simple invoice | 1 |
| Normal multi-page invoice | 2 |
| Complex invoice | 3 |
| Utility statement | 5 |
| Very complex document | 10 |

This allows pricing to follow actual human effort.

# 14. Evidence traceability

Evidence traceability should be **included in the platform**, not sold as a separate premium feature.

The desired chain is:

```text
Source document
    ↓
Invoice/document identifier
    ↓
Page / table / line where available
    ↓
Extracted activity
    ↓
Mapped emission factor
    ↓
Calculation
    ↓
Emission result
    ↓
Report
```

This should become one of CarbonTally's strongest commercial differentiators.

# 16. Billing architecture

The first billing implementation should not reinvent payment infrastructure.

Recommended approach:

**Stripe (or equivalent established billing provider).**

CarbonTally should own:

- customer/product mapping
- subscription state
- entitlements
- processing-credit balances
- usage records
- manual-extraction charges
- billing history references

The payment provider should own:

- card/payment collection
- invoices/payment receipts
- payment retries
- tax/payment mechanisms supported by the provider
- subscription payment lifecycle

# 17. Important unit-economics requirement

Before finalising prices, calculate:

```text
Customer revenue
-
Payment processing
-
Vercel
-
Render
-
Supabase
-
Storage
-
Backups
-
OCR
-
AI/API costs
-
Email
-
Human extraction labour
-
Support
=
Contribution margin
```

This must be measured for:

1. CSV processing.
2. Excel processing.
3. PDF extraction.
4. Image OCR.
5. AI extraction.
6. Human extraction.
7. Storage.
8. Report generation.
9. White-label email/report operations.

Do not permanently promise £0.99 manual extraction until the actual labour cost supports a healthy margin.

# 23. Key commercial principle

CarbonTally should monetise the **work it performs**, not simply the number of people who log in.

The model should therefore be:

```text
                CARBONTALLY
                     │
          ┌──────────┴──────────┐
          │                     │
       PLATFORM             PROCESSING
       SUBSCRIPTION            USAGE
          │                     │
          │             ┌───────┴────────┐
          │             │                │
          │         AUTOMATED          HUMAN
          │         PROCESSING       EXTRACTION
          │
          └──────────────┬──────────────┘

                    + REPORTING
```
