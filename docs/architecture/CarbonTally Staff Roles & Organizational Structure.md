# AI Agents (Digital Employees)

In addition to human staff, CarbonTally can use specialized AI agents:

- **Document Intake Agent** – Validates uploads and routes documents.
- **OCR Processing Agent** – Coordinates OCR and extraction.
- **Data Mapping Agent** – Suggests emission factor mappings.
- **Validation Assistant Agent** – Flags low-confidence records for review.
- **Carbon Calculation Agent** – Runs emissions calculations.
- **Report Generation Agent** – Produces reports and audit packs.
- **Customer Support Agent** – Answers common questions and guides users.
- **Notification Agent** – Sends in-app, email, and workflow notifications.
- **Quality Assurance Agent** – Detects anomalies and potential data quality issues.
- **Compliance Monitoring Agent** – Tracks emission factor and regulatory updates.

These AI agents support the human team by automating repetitive work while keeping humans responsible for quality assurance, customer communication, and final approvals.
