## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Introduction

XYZ (UK) Limited is pleased to present its 2025 Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR) Report. This report provides a comprehensive account of our energy consumption, greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, and energy efficiency activities for the financial year ended 31 December 2025.

### 1.2 Key Highlights

| Metric | 2025 Value | 2024 Value | Change |
|--------|------------|------------|--------|
| Total Energy Consumption | 8,492,000 kWh | 8,510,000 kWh | -0.2% |
| Scope 1 Emissions | 345.2 tCO₂e | 345.0 tCO₂e | +0.1% |
| Scope 2 Emissions | 567.8 tCO₂e | 524.2 tCO₂e | +8.3% |
| Scope 3 Emissions | 321.6 tCO₂e | 337.6 tCO₂e | -4.7% |
| **Total Emissions** | **1,234.6 tCO₂e** | **1,206.8 tCO₂e** | **+2.3%** |
| Emissions Intensity (tCO₂e/£1,000) | 0.024 | 0.026 | -7.7% |

### 1.3 Performance Summary

- **Energy Consumption:** Total energy consumption decreased by 0.2% despite business growth, demonstrating improved energy efficiency.
- **Scope 1 Emissions:** Remained stable with a slight increase of 0.1%, as reductions in fleet fuel were offset by increased natural gas usage.
- **Scope 2 Emissions:** Increased by 8.3% due to expansion of London office and increased data center activity.
- **Scope 3 Emissions:** Decreased by 4.7%, driven by reductions in business travel and waste emissions.
- **Intensity Ratio:** Improved by 7.7%, showing successful decoupling of emissions from business growth.

We have used the **operational control approach** where:
- 100% of emissions from operations we control are included
- Our offices, vehicles, and equipment are under direct control
- Purchased electricity and heat are included as Scope 2
- We do not include emissions from non-controlled operations

## 16. Glossary

### 16.1 Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **CO₂e** | Carbon Dioxide Equivalent - A unit of measurement that converts different greenhouse gases into an equivalent amount of carbon dioxide based on their global warming potential |
| **GHG** | Greenhouse Gases - Gases that trap heat in the atmosphere, including CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, and F-gases |
| **GHG Protocol** | The Greenhouse Gas Protocol - The global standard for corporate greenhouse gas accounting |
| **Intensity Ratio** | A measure of emissions normalized to a business metric, such as turnover or employee count |
| **kWh** | Kilowatt-hour - A unit of energy consumption equal to 1,000 watt-hours |
| **Location-Based Method** | A Scope 2 calculation method that uses the average emission factor of the grid where the electricity is consumed |
| **Market-Based Method** | A Scope 2 calculation method that uses specific emission factors from electricity suppliers or renewable energy certificates |
| **Operational Control** | An organizational boundary definition where a company reports 100% of emissions from operations it controls |
| **Scope 1** | Direct greenhouse gas emissions from sources owned or controlled by the company |
| **Scope 2** | Indirect emissions from the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, or cooling |
| **Scope 3** | All other indirect emissions that occur in the company's value chain |
| **SECR** | Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting - The UK government framework for mandatory reporting of energy consumption and carbon emissions by large companies |
| **tCO₂e** | Tonne of Carbon Dioxide Equivalent - 1,000 kg CO₂e |
| **UK Grid Average** | The average emission factor for electricity generated on the UK national grid |

### 16.3 Emission Factor Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Activity Data** | The quantitative measure of an activity that results in GHG emissions (e.g., kWh of electricity consumed) |
| **Emission Factor** | The average emission rate of a given source, relative to units of activity (e.g., kg CO₂e per kWh) |
| **Global Warming Potential** | A measure of how much energy the emissions of 1 ton of a gas will absorb over a given period, relative to 1 ton of CO₂ |
| **Net Calorific Value** | The amount of heat released by burning a fuel, excluding the latent heat of vaporization of water |
