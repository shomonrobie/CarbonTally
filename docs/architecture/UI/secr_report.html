1. Executive Summary

XYZ (UK) Limited is pleased to present its 2025 Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR) Report. This report provides a comprehensive account of our energy consumption, greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, and energy efficiency activities for the financial year ended 31 December 2025.

Key Highlights:

  • Total energy consumption: 8,492,000 kWh
  • Total Scope 1 emissions: 345.2 tCO₂e (↓ 8.1% from 2024)
  • Total Scope 2 emissions: 567.8 tCO₂e (↑ 2.4% from 2024)
  • Total Scope 3 emissions: 321.6 tCO₂e (↓ 5.7% from 2024)
  • Overall emissions intensity: 0.024 tCO₂e per £1,000 turnover

We have maintained our commitment to reducing environmental impact through targeted energy efficiency measures, including the installation of LED lighting across all UK offices, optimization of fleet routes, and implementation of a smart building management system. These initiatives have contributed to an overall emissions reduction of 5.2% since 2024.

This report has been prepared in accordance with the SECR framework and the UK Government's GHG Conversion Factors for Company Reporting (2025). The data presented herein is supported by a robust audit trail and has been reviewed by our sustainability team and external advisors.

4. Methodology

4.1 Reporting Boundary

This report covers 100% of the energy consumption and emissions from all UK operations, including:

  • Four office locations (London, Manchester, Birmingham, Glasgow)
  • One co-located data center in London
  • Company-owned fleet vehicles
  • All business travel (UK and international)

The operational boundary has been defined using the operational control approach, in line with the GHG Protocol and SECR requirements.

4.2 Emission Factors

12. Energy Efficiency Actions

During the reporting period, we have implemented a comprehensive energy efficiency program across all our operations. The following actions have been taken:

12.1 LED Lighting Retrofit

We completed a full LED lighting retrofit across all UK offices and the data center, replacing 2,400 fluorescent and halogen fixtures with energy-efficient LED alternatives. This project has resulted in a 28% reduction in lighting energy consumption, saving approximately 85,000 kWh annually.

Investment: £48,000 | Payback Period: 2.5 years | Carbon Savings: 17.6 tCO₂e/year

12.2 Smart Building Management System

We installed a smart building management system (BMS) across all offices, providing real-time monitoring and control of heating, ventilation, air conditioning (HVAC), and lighting systems. The BMS uses occupancy sensors and weather forecasting to optimize energy use.

Impact: 12% reduction in HVAC energy consumption, saving 210,000 kWh annually.

12.3 Fleet Electrification

In 2025, we began the transition of our fleet to electric vehicles. By year-end, 3 of 12 vehicles (25%) were fully electric, with charging infrastructure installed at all office locations.

12.4 On-Site Solar Generation

We installed solar panels on the London headquarters roof, with a capacity of 45 kWp. In its first year of operation, the system generated 38,000 kWh of renewable electricity, offsetting 7.9 tCO₂e.

14. Glossary

CO₂e (Carbon Dioxide Equivalent): A unit of measurement that converts different greenhouse gases into an equivalent amount of carbon dioxide based on their global warming potential.

GHG Protocol: The Greenhouse Gas Protocol, the global standard for corporate greenhouse gas accounting.

Intensity Ratio: A measure of emissions normalized to a business metric, such as turnover or employee count.

kWh (Kilowatt-hour): A unit of energy consumption equal to 1,000 watt-hours.

Location-Based Method: A Scope 2 calculation method that uses the average emission factor of the grid where the electricity is consumed.

Operational Control Approach: An organizational boundary definition where a company reports 100% of emissions from operations it controls.

Scope 1: Direct greenhouse gas emissions from sources owned or controlled by the company.

Scope 2: Indirect emissions from the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, or cooling.

Scope 3: All other indirect emissions that occur in the company's value chain.

SECR (Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting): The UK government framework for mandatory reporting of energy consumption and carbon emissions by large companies.

tCO₂e (Tonne of Carbon Dioxide Equivalent): A metric unit of emissions, equal to 1,000 kg CO₂e.
